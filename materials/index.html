№What I cannot show you

No animal-welfare standard covers any yarn I use. Not one. I could point at a certificate that covers something else and let it do the implying for me, and I would rather not.

So this page is the whole of my evidence: the fibres, the percentages, and the ones I have not confirmed yet marked as unconfirmed. When a standard genuinely covers something I buy, it will appear here with its number and its date, and I will say exactly what it does and does not mean.

Until then, the composition above is the claim. Nothing behind it.
