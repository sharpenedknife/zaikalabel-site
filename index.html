Why this shop exists

I started this shop as a protest. I don't believe any living creature should suffer for what someone wears. So I don't work with real fur. The fur here is faux, every time, on purpose.

That conviction is the whole reason this shop exists. It also commits me to being straight about what each piece is actually made of, including the parts that are synthetic. Every fibre is printed in full.

A scarf takes me 11 to 14 hours. I don't rush it.
