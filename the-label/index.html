The label · one maker, Wrocław

Zaika is Zlata.

I am Zlata. I make one piece at a time, by hand, in Wrocław, Poland.

I started this shop as a protest. I don't believe any living creature should suffer for what someone wears. So I don't work with real fur. The fur here is faux, every time, on purpose.

I work in alpaca, alpaca-silk, cloud yarn, cotton-linen. I choose fibres for how they move and feel.

Each piece sells once, to one person, and then it's gone. The listing closes for good.

A scarf takes me 11 to 14 hours. I don't rush it.

To me, every piece sold is a small step toward a world I want to live in. I can't promise it changes much. But it's the step I get to make, and making these is what I want to do with my life.

How the pieces are made
